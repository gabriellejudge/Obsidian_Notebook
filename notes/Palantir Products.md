# Palantir Products

> **Summary**: Overview of Palantir's three core products—Gotham, Foundry, and Apollo—which serve intelligence, defense, and enterprise clients with data integration and analysis capabilities.

## Product Suite

### Gotham
- **Target Market**: Intelligence and defense agencies
- **Function**: Helps analysts connect people, places, and events
- **Use Cases**: Investigations, counterterrorism, military operations

### Foundry
- **Target Market**: Commercial enterprises and government
- **Function**: Data integration and operational analytics platform
- **Use Cases**: Supply chain, manufacturing, financial operations

### Apollo
- **Target Market**: Internal/infrastructure
- **Function**: Mission control for software deployment
- **Capabilities**: Manages updates for Gotham, Foundry, and AIP across cloud-to-edge environments

## Architecture Pattern

```
Apollo (deployment layer)
    ├── Gotham (intelligence/defense)
    ├── Foundry (enterprise)
    └── AIP (AI platform)
```
