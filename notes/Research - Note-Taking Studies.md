# Research: Note-Taking Studies

Collection of research findings on effective note-taking and knowledge management.

## Key Findings

### Generation Effect
Research shows that generating your own summaries leads to better retention than passive highlighting. This supports the practice of writing notes in your own words.

### Spacing Effect  
Reviewing material at spaced intervals improves long-term retention. This is why periodic review of notes is valuable for [[Personal Knowledge Management]].

### Elaborative Interrogation
Asking "why" and "how" questions while learning improves comprehension. This could inform how we structure notes.
