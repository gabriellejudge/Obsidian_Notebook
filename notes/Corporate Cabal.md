# Corporate Cabal

> **Summary**: Reference to research on networks connecting intelligence agencies (CIA) with NGOs and corporate entities. Explores the "liberal CIA" concept and institutional power structures.

## Source Reference

- **Link**: https://isgp-studies.com/liberal-cia-ngo-network

## Key Concept

The "Liberal CIA" refers to the documented historical collaboration between U.S. intelligence agencies and ostensibly liberal or progressive NGOs, foundations, and media organizations during the Cold War and beyond.
