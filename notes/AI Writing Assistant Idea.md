# AI Writing Assistant Idea

What if we could build a writing assistant that doesn't just correct grammar, but actually understands the *intent* behind what you're trying to say?

## The Concept

Most writing tools focus on surface-level improvements:
- Grammar corrections
- Spelling fixes
- Basic style suggestions

But what about understanding context? What about knowing that when I write "the thing" I actually mean "the deployment pipeline" because that's what I've been discussing in my other notes?

## Potential Features

- [ ] Context-aware suggestions based on vault content
- [ ] Learning from my writing patterns over time
- [ ] Suggesting connections to related ideas I've written before
- [ ] Helping develop rough ideas into structured outlines
