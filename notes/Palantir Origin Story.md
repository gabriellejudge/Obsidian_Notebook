# Palantir Origin Story

> **Summary**: Documents the founding connection between Palantir and DARPA's Total Information Awareness (TIA) program. A meeting between Peter Thiel, Alex Karp, and Admiral John Poindexter established the vision that became Palantir.

## The Founding Meeting

**Key Participants:**
- **Peter Thiel** - PayPal co-founder, venture capitalist → [[All Kings]]
- **Alex Karp** - Palantir CEO
- **Admiral John Poindexter** - Head of DARPA's Information Awareness Office

**Context:**
- This meeting occurred when Poindexter was newly appointed to lead DARPA's **Total Information Awareness (TIA)** program
- TIA was defunded in late **2003** due to public concerns about mass surveillance
- Palantir emerged to carry forward similar capabilities in the private sector

## Significance

This origin story reveals:
1. **Direct government-to-startup pipeline** for surveillance technology
2. Palantir as **privatized continuation** of a defunded government program
3. **Peter Thiel's** role bridging Silicon Valley and intelligence community
