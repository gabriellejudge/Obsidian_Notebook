# Personal Knowledge Management

A system for capturing, organizing, and retrieving personal knowledge over time.

## Why PKM Matters

In an age of information overload, having a personal system for managing knowledge isn't optional—it's essential. Without one, we:

- Forget important insights
- Fail to connect related ideas
- Reinvent solutions to problems we've already solved

## My PKM Principles

1. **Capture quickly** - Reduce friction to zero
2. **Process regularly** - Don't let inbox grow indefinitely  
3. **Connect actively** - Link notes to each other
4. **Review periodically** - Resurface old insights

## Tools I Use

- Obsidian for note-taking
- Git for version control
- Automated agents for organization

## Challenges

The biggest challenge is maintenance. It's easy to capture notes but hard to keep them organized. This is why I'm exploring [[AI Writing Assistant Idea]] - to automate the tedious parts.
