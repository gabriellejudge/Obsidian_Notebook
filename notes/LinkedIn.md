# LinkedIn

> **Summary**: Analysis of LinkedIn as more than a professional network—viewing it as a national security asset and media controller that shapes the culture of work.

## Key Claims

1. **National Security Asset**
   - Professional data on hundreds of millions of workers
   - Employment history, connections, skills mapping
   - Valuable intelligence for state and corporate actors

2. **Media Controller**
   - Shapes the "culture of work"
   - Influences professional norms and expectations
   - Controls professional discourse and visibility

## Implications

LinkedIn represents the convergence of:
- **Social networking** (Meta-style data collection)
- **Professional identity** (resumes, skills, career trajectories)
- **Media influence** (content algorithms shaping professional culture)
